SKYHUNT LABS · BETA

Sky Lens

Turn your phone into an experimental aircraft finder. SKYHUNT uses your camera, location and device heading to place nearby live aircraft in roughly the right direction.

This is approximate AR, not a precision navigation tool. Compass drift, receiver coverage and aircraft data latency can move targets away from their true visual position.

SKYHUNT releases

Current version: v3.1.0-beta

v3.1.0-beta — Sky Lens

11 August 2026
  • Introduced SKYHUNT LABS.
  • New experimental camera-based Sky Lens mode.
  • Uses camera, location and device orientation to estimate where nearby live aircraft are in the sky.
  • Live target labels show callsign, aircraft type, altitude and approximate distance.
  • Nearest/in-view target receives a highlighted treatment.
  • Open a target directly in the main live tracker.
  • Clearly labelled BETA because phone compass/orientation and ADS-B timing are approximate.

v3.0.1 — Radar Map Upgrade

11 August 2026
  • Completely redesigned World Radar map presentation.
  • New dark tactical basemap treatment.
  • Animated radar rings and sweep overlay.
  • Glowing aircraft target markers.
  • Floating live radar HUD with target count and scan/reset controls.
  • New aircraft preview bottom sheet when a target is tapped.
  • Open or capture an aircraft directly from the map.
  • Improved mobile map proportions and controls.

v3.0.0 — SKYHUNT

  • Complete rebrand from Flight Roulette to SKYHUNT.

  • New Hunt / Radar / Hangar / Flight ID navigation language.
  • Redesigned collector cards, tracking UI and world radar.
  • All proven live-aircraft functionality retained.
