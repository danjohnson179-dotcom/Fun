v1.2 — Skywire Aviation News

12 August 2026

A new live aviation-news experience built directly into SKYHUNT.

  • New Aviation News area accessible from the bottom navigation.
  • Live global aviation coverage indexed through GDELT.
  • Latest, Airlines, Airports, Aircraft and Safety categories.
  • Built-in aviation news search.
  • Publisher, country and publication-time metadata where available.
  • Article imagery where supplied by the news index.
  • Direct links to the original publisher.
  • Short-lived local caching to avoid unnecessary repeat API requests.
  • No news API key required.

v1.0 — Launch

12 August 2026

Welcome to SKYHUNT — live aircraft discovery built for exploring the real sky around you.

  • Discover — hunt a real live aircraft and jump straight into its tracking view.
  • Global Radar — explore sampled live aircraft on an interactive world radar, inspect targets and capture aircraft directly from the map.
  • Nearby — use your location to discover, map and sort aircraft flying around you.
  • Live Tracking — follow a selected aircraft with available live position, altitude, speed, heading, callsign, registration and aircraft data.
  • Collection — capture aircraft you discover, build a persistent on-device collection, track rarities and record repeat discoveries.
  • Flight ID — see your discovery progress, capture totals, aircraft types and Collection statistics.
  • AI Finder · Beta — describe the aircraft you want in natural language and search sampled live traffic for matching aircraft.
  • Sky Lens · Beta — combine live aircraft data with your camera, location and device orientation to estimate where nearby aircraft are in the sky.
  • Aircraft target cards — inspect available callsign, registration, ICAO hex, aircraft type, altitude, speed and other live details.
  • SKYHUNT Labs — a dedicated home for experimental aircraft-discovery tools including Sky Lens and AI Finder.
  • Live ADS-B data — uses public and third-party aviation data sources with fallback behaviour where supported.
  • Mobile-first interface — touch-first navigation across Home, Radar, Nearby, Collection and Labs.
  • No SKYHUNT account required — Collection data is stored locally on the device, while location and camera access are requested only when required by a feature.
  • Built-in transparency — Terms, Privacy, Data & Sources and Safety information are available in-app, including a clear warning that SKYHUNT is not for navigation.

Welcome to v1.0. This is the first public SKYHUNT release. Internal development builds are intentionally excluded from the public release history.
